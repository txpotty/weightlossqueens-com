    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in Levittown, NY

    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in Levittown

    Male pattern hair loss runs on DHT. In genetically sensitive men, DHT gradually miniaturizes follicles, starting at the temples or crown and moving inward. Finasteride lowers DHT, which slows or stops that shrinking. Minoxidil, on its own path, helps follicles grow longer and thicker.

    Who Men's Treatment Is For

    It suits adult men with a receding hairline, thinning crown or diffuse thinning on top. It works best when there is still visible hair in the thinning areas, because follicles that have been bald for years rarely respond to any medicine. Earlier is better. Sudden, patchy or painful hair loss needs an in-person exam instead. Your photos confirm the pattern.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Levittown

    Finasteride is one 1 mg tablet a day, with or without food. Topical minoxidil is applied to a dry scalp once or twice daily, while low-dose oral minoxidil is an off-label daily pill some providers prescribe instead, typically starting around 1.25 to 2.5 mg. A compounded combination spray is applied once a day. Dutasteride is a capsule taken daily.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Months one and two can bring extra shedding, especially with minoxidil. The early shed is usually a sign follicles are cycling, and it fades. By month six, loss typically slows and density starts to improve. Fuller results show between nine and twelve months.

    Side Effects of Men's Treatment in Levittown

    Finasteride can lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in roughly one to two percent of men in trials. These usually resolve after stopping, and many men who notice them find they fade even while staying on the medicine. Mood changes are rare but should be reported promptly. Scalp itch is the usual topical complaint. Oral minoxidil can cause extra body hair, ankle swelling or a faster heartbeat.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure and every medication you take. Expect questions about mood history, libido and family planning, because each can change the plan. PSA readings drop on finasteride. Tell any doctor who checks your prostate that you take it.

    How Men's Options Compare in Levittown

    Finasteride plus minoxidil is the standard starting pair for men. When finasteride is not enough, providers may move to dutasteride, which is off-label for hair. Minoxidil with GHK-Cu is a topical-only blend for men who want to skip DHT blockers. Transplants help some men.
