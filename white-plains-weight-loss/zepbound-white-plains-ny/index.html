    How Zepbound Treatment Works in White Plains, NY

    How Zepbound Works in White Plains

    Tirzepatide acts on two gut hormone receptors. By activating both the GIP and GLP-1 receptors, it slows stomach emptying, signals fullness to the brain and helps the body manage blood sugar after eating. Most people feel full on smaller portions. It is injected under the skin of the stomach, thigh or upper arm once a week.

    Who Zepbound Is For

    Zepbound is approved for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher. Adults with a BMI of 27 or more also qualify if they have a condition tied to weight, such as high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, high cholesterol or sleep apnea. In late 2024 the FDA added moderate to severe obstructive sleep apnea in adults with obesity. It is not approved for children.

    Zepbound Dosing Schedule in White Plains

    Everyone starts at 2.5 mg. That first month is about letting your stomach adjust, not about weight loss, so modest early changes are normal. After four weeks the dose usually moves up in 2.5 mg steps to 5, 7.5, 10, 12.5 and up to 15 mg, with at least four weeks at each. Most people settle at 5 mg, 10 mg or 15 mg.

    What to Expect Week by Week in White Plains

    The first month is mostly quiet. You may notice a smaller appetite within the first week, and stomach upset tends to cluster in the day or two after a shot or a dose increase. The SURMOUNT-1 clinical study reported average weight loss of roughly 20 percent at the 15 mg dose over 72 weeks. Individual results vary.

    Zepbound Side Effects

    Nausea and diarrhea come up most. Vomiting, constipation, indigestion, stomach pain and redness at the injection site are also common, especially during the first days after each dose increase. Serious but less common risks include pancreatitis, gallbladder problems, kidney injury from dehydration and severe allergic reactions. Zepbound carries a boxed warning about thyroid C-cell tumors seen in rodents.

    Eligibility and Screening in White Plains

    Your provider starts with height, weight and BMI. Next comes your medical history and every medication you take, with special attention to insulin, sulfonylureas and birth control pills. Anyone with medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2 in their family is not a candidate, and Zepbound is not used while pregnant. Labs may be requested.

    Zepbound Compared With Related Options in White Plains

    Zepbound and Mounjaro are the same medicine. The only real difference is the approved use, weight for Zepbound and type 2 diabetes for Mounjaro, which affects coverage and paperwork. In the SURMOUNT-5 head-to-head trial, tirzepatide produced more average weight loss than Wegovy. Compounded tirzepatide is not FDA-approved and is a different product from Zepbound.
