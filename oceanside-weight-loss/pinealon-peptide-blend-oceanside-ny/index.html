    How Pinealon Works in Oceanside

    Pinealon is a tripeptide, glutamic acid plus aspartic acid plus arginine, developed by Russian researchers who study short bioregulator peptides that may influence gene activity in specific tissues. Lab work suggests it may protect nerve cells from oxidative stress. Human data is thin. The blend adds companion short peptides chosen by the pharmacy and your provider, and none of it is FDA-approved.

    Who Usually Asks About the Pinealon Blend

    Typical candidates are adults over 40 who notice slower recall, afternoon fog or lighter sleep, and who have already tried regular exercise and a consistent bedtime without much change.
